Hello, my cohorts...
I’ve been on social media since I was 14. After years of wandering around this metaverse, there came a time when I realized I couldn't be my true self anymore, which left me with a long period of quiet cognitive dissonance. So, I built this space to hold my daily quotes and thoughts, the ones I couldn't share, even with those of you in my close-friends circle. I was terrified of being vulnerable, yet I still longed for recognition, and along the way, I let friendships fall apart simply because I couldn't say what I truly meant. If you ever stumble upon this, please just scroll, read, and leave it be.
